Privacy Policy

Velvet: Macros & Calories

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Summary

Velvet keeps everything you log on your iPhone, and we do not collect it.

  • There are no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, and no tracking.
  • Your food log, workouts, tracked routes, weight, steps, water, goals, and profile stay in the app's storage on your phone.
  • Looking up a food sends a barcode or search words to a public food database. Your name, profile, and log are never included, though those services can see your IP address, as any website can.
  • The camera is used only to read barcodes, and motion is used only to count steps. Both are processed on your phone.
  • Location is used only while you track a walk, run, or ride, and stays on your phone.
  • Apple Health is optional. If you allow it, Velvet can read your heart rate and save the activities you track to Apple Health.
  • If you turn on rest-over notifications, they are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Anything you export, back up, or share leaves the app only where you choose to send it.
  • Velvet is for adults 18 and over.

Who we are

Velvet: Macros & Calories ("Velvet" or "the app") is a free iPhone app made by Chase Graves, an independent developer based in Los Angeles, California, United States.

For any privacy question, write to downloadvelvetapp@gmail.com.

What the app keeps on your phone

Velvet saves the following in the app's private storage on your iPhone. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us.

  • Foods you log, with amounts, meals, Calories, protein, carbs, and fat, plus foods and units you create yourself.
  • Workouts and training sessions: exercises, sets, weights, reps, durations, and Calorie estimates.
  • Walks, runs, and rides you track: the route (GPS points), distance, time, pace, elevation, and Calorie estimates.
  • Body weight entries, daily step counts, and water intake.
  • Your profile (sex, age, height, weight, activity level, and goal), used only on your phone to estimate daily Calorie and macro targets, plus any goals you set yourself.
  • App settings, such as your color theme, app icon, and workout preferences.

The app uses this information only to show your diary, charts, records, routes, and goals.

What leaves your phone

Velvet has no accounts and no database of its own. The only network requests it makes are food lookups, and the map tiles Apple Maps draws.

  • Barcode scans. When you scan a barcode, the app sends that barcode number to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), an open, community-run food database, to get the product's name and nutrition. As Open Food Facts asks, these requests identify the app by name and version and include our support email address.
  • Food search. When you search for a food by name, the app sends your search words to USDA FoodData Central (fdc.nal.usda.gov), a service of the U.S. Department of Agriculture, and to FatSecret (fatsecret.com), a food and nutrition database. Opening a result may request that food's details. These requests include access keys that belong to the app, not to you. FatSecret requests pass through a small relay run by the developer that holds the app's FatSecret credentials; it forwards your search words and returns the results, and it does not store them.
  • What those services can see. Like any website, they receive your device's IP address along with the request. We never send them your name, profile, weight, food log, workouts, or location. We don't control how they keep their own request logs; see their privacy policies at https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy (Open Food Facts), https://www.usda.gov/privacy-policy (USDA), and https://www.fatsecret.com/privacy (FatSecret).
  • Apple Maps. To draw the map for a route you tracked, the app asks Apple Maps for map pictures of that area. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy. Routes are drawn on your phone, and we never receive them.
  • Apple. If you have allowed iOS to share crash and usage data with developers, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated reports. They contain no personal information about you.

The app sends no analytics, crash reports, or advertising data anywhere else.

Location

If you start tracking a walk, run, or ride, Velvet asks for permission to use your location. It uses it only to measure distance, pace, elevation, and your route, and keeps tracking while your screen is locked or the app is in the background until you pause or finish. When no activity is being tracked, Velvet does not use your location. Your route stays in the app on your phone. It is never sent to us. If you share an activity card, the route picture is included only if you choose to share it. You can turn location off in iOS Settings under Velvet; tracking then will not be available.

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional. If you allow it, Velvet can read your heart rate to show with the walks, runs, rides, and workouts you track, and can save those activities to Apple Health. This information stays on your phone and in your Apple Health, under your control. It is never sent to us, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with anyone. You can change or withdraw access at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings.

Camera, motion, Photos, and notifications

  • Camera. Used only to read barcodes inside the app. Images are processed on your phone to read the code. They are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Motion. Used only to read your step count from your iPhone's motion sensor. Steps are stored in the app on your phone.
  • Photos. If you save a share card or image to your Photos, Velvet asks for permission to add images only. It cannot see or read the photos already in your library.
  • Notifications. If you turn on the rest-over notification, Velvet asks permission to send notifications. It is scheduled on your phone to tell you when a rest timer ends, and it is cancelled when you return to the app. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Lock screen. The rest-over notification can show an exercise name and set number on your lock screen. While you track an activity, a Live Activity on your lock screen and in the Dynamic Island can show your distance and time. You can hide notification previews in iOS Settings > Notifications > Show Previews, and you can turn the notification off in Velvet's Settings > Workouts.

You can turn any of these off in iOS Settings under Velvet. The app still works without them, but barcode scanning, step counting, saving images to Photos, route tracking, or the rest-over notification will not be available.

Sharing, exports, and backups

Velvet can make pictures (such as a daily recap, workout, or route card) and files of your data, such as CSV exports and a backup file. They are created on your phone and handed to the iOS share sheet only when you tap Share, Export, or Back up. Where they go next, whether Messages, Instagram, Files, AirDrop, or Mail, is your choice, and those apps have their own privacy policies. A backup file contains your data, so keep it somewhere you trust.

Cards have privacy options. Your body weight appears on a card only if you turn that option on, and your profile details never appear. The app deletes its temporary copies once the share sheet closes.

Retention and deleting your data

  • How long it stays. Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. Deleting an entry removes it from the app, and deleting the app removes all of its data from your phone.
  • iPhone backups. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Velvet's data may be included in that backup. Those backups are kept by you and Apple under your Apple account, not by us.
  • No copy with us. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot look it up, change it, or delete it for you, and we cannot restore it if you lose your phone without a backup or a backup file from Velvet.

Health information

Weight, food, workout, step, route, and heart rate information is health-related. In Velvet it stays on your phone under the rules above.

Calorie, macro, and Calorie-burn numbers are estimates, and goals are suggestions based on the profile you enter. Velvet is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before making major changes to your diet or exercise.

Age

Velvet is for adults 18 and over. It asks for your age when you set up and does not create targets for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If how the app handles data changes, for example if we add accounts or a server that stores your data, we will update this policy and its date before that change ships. The current version is always available from the Privacy Policy link in the app and on its App Store page.
