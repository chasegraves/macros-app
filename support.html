Support

Velvet: Macros & Calories

Contact

For questions, bug reports, or ideas, write to downloadvelvetapp@gmail.com.

To help track a problem down, please include:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version,
  • the Velvet version (Settings, then About, inside the app),
  • what you did and what happened,
  • a screenshot, if you can. Leave out anything personal you would rather not share.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. Velvet has no accounts and no server. See the Privacy Policy for details.

I got a new phone. How do I move my data? Restore your iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, which brings Velvet's data with it. Because there is no account, there is no other way to move it. You can also export a CSV file from Settings, then Export data, which is a readable copy of your history but cannot be imported back.

Steps are not updating. In iOS Settings, open Velvet and make sure Motion & Fitness is turned on.

The barcode scanner will not open. In iOS Settings, open Velvet and make sure Camera is turned on.

A food is missing or looks wrong. Food data comes from Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central and can be incomplete. You can add your own foods in the app. Open Food Facts is community-edited, so a missing or incorrect product can also be fixed there.

How do I delete my data? Delete entries inside the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your phone. We hold no copy.

Is this medical advice? No. Calorie and nutrition numbers are estimates. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before making major changes to your diet or exercise.

See also the Privacy Policy.
